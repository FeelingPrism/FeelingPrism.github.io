My Interests

A brief overview of my interests and passions about anything.

Engineering

Automotive and Aviation

I have a love for cars and the automotive industry with my favourite being the Mazda MX-5. This is due to Mazda's use of its Jinbai Ittai philosophy of the car and the driver being one, this creates an excellent driving feel for the driver due to the MX-5 being very lightweight and nimble allowing it to be very fun and at a low cost.

My love also extends to Aviation as I have always been fascinated about the complexity of airliners like the A380 and 787 and how engineers design objects to be as aerodynamic, fuel efficient and lightweight as possible and how global transportation truly works.

Military

Defense

I have an interest about the defense sector throughout the world and especially in aviation and armoured vehicle. I am particularly fascinated about vehicles like the Su27 Flanker and how it got developed into other aircraft like the Su-34 and Su-30.

I also have researched on the history of tank armour and its shift from small steel plates to block machine gun fire to rolled homogenous armour in WWII to composite armour and ERA and now using slat armour or APS to combat cheap drones and explosive weapons from destroying million dollar equipment.
