My Interests

A technical overview of my engagement with mechanical and digital systems, focusing on the engineering principles behind modern aerospace, automotive, and defense platforms.

Engineering

Automotive & Aviation

I have a deep passion for the automotive industry, particularly the Mazda MX-5. I am fascinated by Mazda's "Jinba Ittai" philosophy of the car and driver as one. This focus on lightweight, nimble engineering creates a pure driving experience that prioritizes mechanical feel over raw horsepower.

My interest extends to Aviation, where I study the complexity of airliners like the A380 and 787. I’m interested in how engineers optimize massive and complex structures to be as aerodynamic and fuel-efficient as possible, pushing the limits of global transportation and material science.

Military

Defense Systems

I follow the global defense sector closely, specifically regarding aviation and armored vehicles. I am particularly interested in the Su-27 Flanker family and its evolution into modern multi-role platforms like the Su-30 and Su-34.

I also research the history of tank armor, from early steel plates to the development of Composite Armor and ERA. I have been fascinated by modern countermeasures like Slat armor and Active Protection Systems (APS) designed to protect high-value assets from modern battlefield threats like cheap drones and explosive weaponry

Technology

Hardware Optimization

I have a constant urge to modify and optimize any tech I own. Whether it's my PC or smartphone, I look for ways to tweak the software for performance gains and greater OS freedom as I do not believe in "stock" configurations if a better one is possible.

I frequently switch between OOS and custom ROMs like AxionOS and LineageOS to experiment with kernel-level changes. I enjoy testing builds for the community and extending the lifecycle of older devices, like my Mi9T, through updated security patches and custom kernels.

Photography

Technical Photography

I use my OnePlus 13R as a primary tool for mobile photography, focusing on capturing the mechanical details and environments I encounter. I am interested in the post-processing and color science that turns a quick snap into a professional-grade image.
